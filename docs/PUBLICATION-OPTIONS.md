## Executive Summary & Strategy Overview

For a student researcher operating under a strict **ZERO-BUDGET ($0)** constraint:
- **Best Route (Rank #1)**: Post preprint on **IEEE TechRxiv** ($0) and submit manuscript to a **Traditional IEEE Subscription Journal** (e.g., IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society / IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems) choosing the **Traditional Subscription Option** ($0 APC).
- **Backup Route (Rank #2)**: Deposit manuscript in **arXiv / Zenodo** ($0) and submit to **University Institutional Repository** ($0).
- **Avoid (Category B)**: Gold Open Access journals like **IEEE Access** ($0 to submit, but requires $2,075 Article Processing Charge after acceptance).
- **Reject (Category D)**: Unverified third-party journals claiming guaranteed acceptance or fast 48-hour reviews.

## Category A: TRUE ZERO-COST SUBMISSION + PUBLICATION ($0 Total)

### OPTION 1 (Rank #1 — Recommended)
- **VENUE**: IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society (ITTS) / IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems (THMS) [Traditional Non-OA Track]
- **OFFICIAL URL**: https://ts.ieee.org/ / https://www.ieee-smc.org/publications/transactions-on-human-machine-systems/
- **TYPE**: Traditional Subscription-Based IEEE Journal
- **COST**: **$0** (No submission fee, no publication fee under traditional subscription model; libraries pay IEEE Xplore subscription).
- **DEADLINE**: Rolling submission (no fixed deadline).
- **ELIGIBILITY**: Global student and professional researchers.
- **FORMAT**: IEEE Transactions 2-column format (LaTeX / Word).
- **REVIEW**: Double-blind / Single-blind peer review (3–6 months).
- **INDEXING**: IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Web of Science (SCI/SCIE).
- **PROS**: 100% free under traditional option; highest academic credibility; indexed in IEEE Xplore & Scopus; allowed preprint posting on TechRxiv.
- **CONS**: Peer review timeline is longer (3–6 months); strict page limits before overlength charges kick in (>8–10 pages).
- **RISK**: High review rigor; requires faculty advisor co-authorship for maximum acceptance probability.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: **VERIFIED — BEST OPTION FOR ZERO BUDGET**

- **OFFICIAL URL**: https://www.techrxiv.org/

- **COST**: **$0** (100% free to submit, host, and download).

- **RECOMMENDATION**: **VERIFIED — RECOMMENDED PREPRINT STEP BEFORE JOURNAL SUBMISSION**

## Researcher Identity & Prerequisites

To submit to traditional IEEE journals or TechRxiv, the student author should prepare:

1. **ORCID iD**: Free digital persistent identifier ([orcid.org](https://orcid.org)).
2. **IEEE Account**: Free login account for IEEE manuscript submission systems ([ieee.org](https://ieee.org)).
3. **Institutional Email**: University `.edu` / `.ac.in` email address.
4. **Faculty Advisor Co-Authorship**: Strongly recommended for peer-reviewed IEEE Transactions.

---

## Copyright & Preprint Policy Rules (IEEE Official Policy)

- **Prior to Acceptance**: Authors have the explicit legal right to post the initial submitted manuscript on **IEEE TechRxiv**, **arXiv**, or their personal/institutional website.
- **Upon Acceptance**: Authors must add the IEEE copyright notice to the preprint.
- **Post Publication**: Upon publication in IEEE Xplore, the author updates the preprint with a link to the official Version of Record (VOR) DOI.
